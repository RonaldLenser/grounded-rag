## Failures and judge notes

- `rmf-robustness-definition`: claim partially_supported: "The AI RMF defines robustness or generalizability as the ability of a system to maintain its level of performance under a variety of circumstances." (The first quote gives the definition of robustness or generalizability as the ability to maintain performance under a variety of circumstances, but it attributes that definition to ISO/IEC TS 5723:2022 rather than showing the AI RMF itself defining it, so the claim's attribution to the AI RMF is not directly established by the quotes.)
- `rmf-risk-response-options`: claim partially_supported: "MANAGE 1.3 lists mitigating, transferring, avoiding, or accepting as risk response options." (The quotes state that risk response options can include mitigating, transferring, avoiding, or accepting risk, which matches the claim's list, but they do not explicitly tie this list to the MANAGE 1.3 subcategory label, which is only implied by the second quote's reference to high-priority risk responses being developed and documented.)
- `rmf-ai-vs-software-risks`: claim partially_supported: "Data used to build an AI system may not truly or appropriately represent the context or intended use, and harmful bias and other data quality issues can affect trustworthiness." (The quotes clearly state that training data may not be a true or appropriate representation of the context or intended use and that datasets may become stale or detached from context, but none of them explicitly mention harmful bias or state that data quality issues affect trustworthiness, though bias management is referenced only in relation to pre-trained models.)
- `genai-red-team-composition`: claim partially_supported: "Combining expert and general public participants can help when it is difficult to recruit specialists, and may expand coverage of the AI risk attack surface." (The quotes state that red-teaming may combine expert and general public participants and that such approaches may expand coverage of the AI risk attack surface, but nothing in the quotes says combining them helps when it is difficult to recruit specialists.)
- `multi-harmful-bias`: claim partially_supported: "The GAI Profile describes bias as mutually reinforcing with undesired homogenization, where overly uniform outputs can amplify harmful biases and flow from foundation models to downstream systems." (The quotes confirm that bias is described as mutually reinforcing with undesired homogenization and that these phenomena can flow from foundation models to downstream systems, but they do not explicitly state that overly uniform outputs amplify harmful biases.)

## Method

- Golden set: `evals/golden.jsonl`, hand-written from the two NIST PDFs. Every expected page was checked to contain the answer; every unanswerable question was checked by text search to be absent from both documents.
- Each question is sent once to `/search` (top 6, for retrieval and gate metrics) and once to `/query` (the real answer path), over HTTP against the target.
- Judge verdicts come from an LLM and can be wrong. Each one includes a reason below or in the JSON file; spot-check before quoting numbers.
- Small sample: one question is 4% of the answerable set. Treat differences of one or two questions as noise.
